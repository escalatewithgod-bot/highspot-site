What HighSpot actually is

A small, honest experiment — not a company, not an investment, just a fun test of internet attention.

🎯 The idea

HighSpot is a pay-to-rank leaderboard. Anyone can bid to put their TikTok, Instagram, or link on the board — outbid the current #1 to take the top spot, or come in under it and still land a seat further down. It's a lighthearted riff on the internet's obsession with rankings and attention, built as a small side project.

💳 How bidding works

Any bid, however small, gets you a real spot on the board — you don't need to beat #1 to appear, only to become #1.

📊 The stats are real

Every number on this site reflects something that actually happened — nothing here is randomized or invented to make the site look busier than it is:

"Online" countlive, real-time
"Visitors since launch"real, counted once per visit
Click counts per listingreal, only rise on actual taps
Bids and rankingsreal, processed through Stripe

If a number looks unimpressive some days, that's because it's genuinely small some days — that's the deal.

📣 How this gets found

Right now, the only promotion is me posting about it on TikTok — @klicpk.

🚫 What's not allowed

Links to adult content are automatically blocked. Beyond that, use good judgment — this is meant to be a fun, low-stakes experiment, not a place for anything harmful.
