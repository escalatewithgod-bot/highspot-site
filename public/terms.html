Terms of Service

last updated August 2026

What this is

HighSpot is a pay-to-rank leaderboard. You submit a handle or link, pick a bid amount, and pay through Stripe to appear on the board. Outbidding the current #1 takes their spot; any bid still gets you a seat further down.

Payments

All payments are processed by Stripe. Bids are generally final — since your spot on the board is the thing you're paying for, and that's delivered immediately once payment succeeds. If something's gone wrong with a charge, contact us and we'll sort it out.

No guaranteed ranking or duration

Anyone can outbid you at any time. We don't guarantee you'll stay at #1, or at any particular rank, for any length of time.

Content rules

  • No links to adult content — these are automatically blocked, but we may also remove listings manually.
  • No illegal content, harassment, or anything designed to harm someone.
  • We can remove any listing that violates these rules, without a refund of the bid.

Age requirement

You must be old enough in your jurisdiction to enter into this kind of agreement and to use Stripe's payment services.

No warranty

This site is run as a small independent project. It's provided as-is, without guarantees about uptime, accuracy of stats, or anything else. Use it for what it is — a fun, low-stakes experiment.
