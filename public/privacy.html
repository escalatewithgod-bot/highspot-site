Privacy Policy

last updated August 2026

What we collect

Only what you choose to submit when you place a bid — all of it already public on the board itself:

Handle or linkwhat you type in
Taglineoptional, what you type in
Photooptional, if you choose to upload one
Click counthow many times your listing was tapped

Payments

Handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number, expiry, or CVC — that information goes directly to Stripe, not to us.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for your name, email, or address to use the board. We don't use tracking cookies or run ads. A little bit of browser storage (localStorage/sessionStorage) is used only to remember your own past bids on your own device, so we can show you a "bid again" prompt if you get outbid.

Who else touches this data

  • Stripe — processes payments.
  • Supabase — hosts our database and stores uploaded photos.
  • Netlify — hosts the website itself.

None of these get anything beyond what's needed to run the site.

Want something removed?

Email us and we'll take down your listing, photo, or any data tied to it.
